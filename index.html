Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Gilded Gryphon Studios LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates Stolas. This Privacy Policy explains how Stolas handles information provided when you use the app.

Information We Use

Stolas allows you to create profiles containing:

  • Name
  • Birth date
  • Birth time
  • Birth location

This information is used solely to calculate and display astrology charts, insights, transits, compatibility information, and other related features within the app.

How We Handle Your Information

Stolas does not sell your personal information and does not share your profile information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your profile information is not transmitted to our servers. It is used locally on your device to provide the app's astrology features.

Google Play Purchases

Stolas uses Google Play Billing to process subscriptions and determine access to Premium features. Stolas receives purchase and subscription information from Google Play for this purpose.

Stolas does not receive or store your payment card information or other payment credentials. Payment information is handled by Google Play and is subject to Google's privacy policies and terms.

Purchase and subscription information is used solely to provide and manage Premium functionality within Stolas. Gilded Gryphon Studios LLC does not sell or use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Profile information remains on your device until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app. Gilded Gryphon Studios LLC does not maintain a server-side copy of your profile information.

Data Security

Because profile information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database of this information. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting it from unauthorized access.

Children's Privacy

Stolas is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
